A run is thirty waves across nine arenas. Enemies spawn out at the rim and come
for you; a wave is over when the last one dies, not when the last one spawns.
Every few waves the arena crossfades into a new one — new colours, new obstacle
layout, new hazards, a new signature enemy. Touching an enemy hurts, so the two
things that kill new players are standing still and forgetting that.

Every fifth cleared wave the run pauses and asks whether to open the shop or
push straight on. The spawner waits but your drone still flies, so it is also
your chance to sweep up loose credits. The shop covers eight upgrade lines —
Hull Plating, Shield Capacitor, Aux Thruster, Overclock, Heavy Rounds, Twin
Barrel, Long Barrel and Ricochet Coils — plus one item slot and one consumable
slot. Hull does not regenerate on its own, so treat every point as spent until
the next shop.

Waves 10, 20 and 30 are boss waves: a capital drone themed to its arena,
summoning escorts on a timer, and the wave does not end until it dies. Killing
one offers a choice of three actives; later bosses upgrade what you hold or
offer something you don't. At half health the final boss tears the arena open
into the Singularity, a black-hole void you finish the fight in.

Clear the arc and you can prestige: start again from wave one stripped of every
upgrade and item, in exchange for a permanent hull, speed and damage bonus that
stacks up to five levels and persists between sessions. A second ship — a fast
gatling build, weaker per shot — unlocks once your lifetime score across all
runs is high enough. Runs can be saved from the pause menu.
